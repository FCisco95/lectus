## What Lectus does

Lectus converts speech to text and can optionally clean up, translate or send the resulting text to a paired computer. Output is generated by models and may be inaccurate, incomplete or in the wrong language. Review important content before relying on or sending it. The software does not provide professional medical, legal or financial advice.
