Privacy

The short version: speech is turned into text on your own computer, and nothing is sent to a server unless you switch a cloud option on.

This page is a plain summary. The full privacy notice is being finalised and will be published here before the first public release. The app shows you the full notice during setup.

What stays on your computer

  • Your audio. It is held in memory while it is turned into text and is not saved as a file.
  • Your dictation history, up to the last 500 dictations.
  • Your settings, vocabulary and replacement rules.

What goes online

  • Model downloads. Speech and clean-up models are downloaded from a model hosting service such as Hugging Face.
  • Update checks. Lectus asks GitHub whether a newer version exists and downloads it from there.
  • Membership checks. Lectus confirms your membership with external services.
  • Cloud options you enable. Cloud speech, cloud clean-up and translation send that request to the service you chose. They are off by default.
  • Paired computers. When you pair two computers, the finished text travels between them on your local network, encrypted.

Those services receive normal network information such as your IP address.

This website

This site sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads no files from other servers. Download links point to GitHub, which has its own privacy terms.
