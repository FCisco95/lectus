Hold the key, say it, let go.

  1. Hold Right Ctrl

    The small Lectus orb opens and starts listening. It keeps the half second before your key press, so the first word is never cut off.

  2. Say what you mean

    Talk at your normal pace. The coloured bars follow your voice so you know it hears you. Music and videos are muted while you speak.

  3. Let go

    Lectus turns the recording into text on your computer and types it where your cursor is. No window to switch to, nothing to paste.

Clean-up that drops the ums

Switch on AI clean-up and Lectus fixes punctuation, capitals and spacing, and removes filler words. It is told never to translate, summarise or answer what you said, so your meaning stays put.

Choose a tone: neutral, formal, casual or concise. Clean-up can run on your computer with a local model, or with a cloud service you connect yourself.

Speak at one computer. Type on another.

Pair two computers on the same network with a code. Dictate into the microphone on your PC and the finished text is typed on the other machine.

  • Only text travels, never audio.
  • It is encrypted and goes straight from one computer to the other, with no server in between.
  • The receiving computer keeps its own microphone off.

Works between a Windows PC and an Apple silicon Mac.

A speech API on your own machine.

Turn on the local API and your scripts and tools can send a WAV file and get text back. It listens only on this computer, needs a token, and uses the local model only.

  • Bound to 127.0.0.1, off by default.
  • Clips up to 120 seconds, 30 requests a minute.
  • Never touches the microphone, your history or the cloud.

Questions people ask first

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Lectus needs the internet once, to download the speech model during setup. After that, dictation runs on your computer with no connection. Cloud options are separate and stay off unless you enable them.

Which apps can I dictate into?

Any app where you can click into a text field and type: mail, chat, documents, browsers, code editors. Lectus sends the text as keystrokes, and uses the clipboard for apps that do not accept them.

What does my PC need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a microphone, and a graphics driver with Vulkan support. Current NVIDIA, AMD and Intel drivers include it. You do not need administrator rights.

Why does Windows warn me when I open the setup file?

Lectus is new and is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, check the file is the Lectus setup you downloaded, then choose Run anyway.

If Smart App Control is on, Windows blocks every unsigned app and offers no way past. The install guide explains your options.

Is my audio stored or sent anywhere?

By default, no. The recording lives in memory while it is turned into text, and is not saved as a file. The resulting text goes into your dictation history on your own computer. Audio leaves your machine only if you turn on a cloud speech option.

Is there a Mac version?

A build for Apple silicon Macs exists and is still being finished. Windows is the version we recommend today. The download page has the current state.

How do I change the shortcut?

Open Settings and choose Dictation. You can hold a single modifier key such as Right Ctrl, use a two-key combination, or use F13 to F15 if your keyboard has them. A second key can start and stop dictation with one press each.
