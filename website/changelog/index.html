0.7.1

11 October 2026

Latest

Dictate on your PC, type on your Mac

Speak into the microphone on your Windows PC and the finished text is inserted on a paired Mac.

  • Pair with a code. In Settings, choose Receive on the Mac and copy its connection code to Send on Windows.
  • They find each other. The two computers connect over your local network, including after an IP address changes.
  • Encrypted text only. No audio is sent. Receive mode turns the local microphone off.
  • Nothing lost. A failed delivery is shown, and the dictation stays in your history on Windows.

Both computers need this version. Release files on GitHub
